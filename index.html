The System

How Elo Works

800
Starting Rating
Every fighter enters with 800 Elo. Beat a stronger fighter, gain more points. Lose to a weaker one, lose more. The system self-calibrates — a fighter's rating reflects the true quality of their opposition, not just their win-loss record.
D=600
Scale Parameter
MMA has far more upsets than chess. Standard Elo uses D=400; we use D=600, meaning a 400-point gap translates to ~82% win probability — not 91%. This flatter curve keeps ratings meaningful even after shocking upsets.
K=40
Adaptive K-Factor
Newer fighters have high K (large swings), established veterans have lower K (stable ratings). K decays by half every 10 fights, settling at a floor of K=22. Win streaks amplify gains up to +15% for elite streaks.
